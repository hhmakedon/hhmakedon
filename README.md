<h2>Brief Bio</h2>
<p>
I’m a computer science and technology teacher in Chicago. My academic background includes degrees in History, Philosophy, Logic and the Philosophy of Science, and Education, which shape how I approach teaching and leadership with both analytical precision and a humanistic perspective. I began my career as a Grade Center teacher during the remote learning era and now focus on designing project-based STEM curriculum for K–8 students. As a first-generation American with Kurdish roots, I draw on my personal history to connect with students from diverse backgrounds and inspire them to pursue excellence. I’m passionate about civic engagement, political theory, ancient philosophy, and a bunch of other subjects. Outside the classroom, I enjoy bringing intensity and focus into my work and life.
</p>
